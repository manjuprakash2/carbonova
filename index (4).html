Leadership

Experience and expertise in activated carbon manufacturing

Manjunath Madihalli Prakash

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Coming from a farming family in Tiptur, Karnataka — home to Asia's largest copra market and world-famous for coconut production — Manjunath brings both heritage and expertise to activated carbon manufacturing. His deep understanding of coconut shell sourcing, combined with extensive experience in supply chain management and strategic planning, creates a unique foundation for Carbonova's quality-focused approach.

With a background spanning demand forecasting, capacity planning, and international logistics across maritime and industrial sectors, Manjunath combines technical expertise with business acumen to drive Carbonova's vision of delivering world-class activated carbon to global markets.

His farmer's roots in Tiptur — the heart of India's coconut industry — ensure authentic understanding of raw material quality and sustainable sourcing practices. This connection, paired with his commitment to traceability and customer partnership, positions Carbonova as a reliable alternative to both high-cost Western suppliers and inconsistent alternatives, offering the perfect balance of international quality standards with competitive Indian manufacturing advantages.
