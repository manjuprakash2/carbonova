About Carbonova

Carbonova is a premium activated carbon manufacturer and exporter based in India, specializing in food-grade, pharmaceutical-grade, and gold recovery activated carbon.

Coming from a farming family in Tiptur, Karnataka — home to Asia's largest copra market and world-famous for coconut production — our founder brings both heritage and expertise to activated carbon manufacturing.

We serve industries worldwide including food processing, pharmaceutical manufacturing, and gold mining operations across Europe, North America, Middle East, and Africa.

Key Features:

  • 100% Coconut Shell Based
  • Food Grade: Iodine 900-1100 mg/g
  • Pharma Grade: Iodine 1000-1200 mg/g
  • Gold Recovery Grade Available
  • ISO Certified Quality
  • Full Traceability & Documentation
  • Export to 4 Continents
